A **web designer** and developer from Italy, these days an **Engineering Manager** and Tech Lead. I spent 2021-2023 leading a **DeFi** team, went all-in on **Node** around the same time, and never looked back: **Vue**, **Nuxt**, **Hono**, and as little framework as the job needs.

Old-school gamer, sometime photographer, and, after a 2020-lockdown detour into **Blender**, a maker of GMK keycaps. This site is my portfolio, but also a scratch space. So yeah: a blog, but not a blog.
